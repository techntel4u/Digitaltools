01
Google Classroom~10 min

Gemini in Classroom

You'll make: a starter resource for your next topic, such as a quiz, a lesson hook or a list of common misconceptions, built from your own material.

  1. Go to classroom.google.com and click the Gemini tab in the navigation bar.
  2. Pick a tool, for example generate a quiz, craft a hook, address common misconceptions or create an interactive activity.
  3. Tell it your topic and your learners' level, for example "Level 2 Electrical Installation, safe isolation". Add your own notes or a file if you can.
  4. Read the output critically. Edit anything that's wrong or doesn't fit your learners.
  5. Save it to Drive or use it in an assignment.

Can't see the Gemini tab? The tools your account shows depend on what the college has switched on. Try tile 02 instead.

Inclusion challenge

Ask Gemini for a second version of what it made: a lower reading age, a glossary of key terms, or sentence starters. Which learner would use it?

Stretch

Turn the quiz into a Practice Set with a hint on the hardest question.

02
Wayground~10 min

Quiz

You'll make: an assessment for one of your groups, written by AI from a topic or pulled from a worksheet you already use, then checked by you.

Option A: from a topic

  1. Log in at wayground.com with your college email.
  2. Click Create → Assessment.
  3. Enter a topic, or paste in text you want the questions based on.
  4. Choose the level, number of questions and output language, then click Create resource.
  5. Review every question. Edit, delete or add, then click Publish.

Option B: from a worksheet or past paper

  1. Click Create → Assessment → Generate with AI → Document.
  2. Upload a PDF, Word file, PowerPoint or a photo of a worksheet.
  3. Review the questions it pulls out and click Publish.

Fastest of all: click Library, search your topic, and Save an existing quiz to edit.

Inclusion challenge

When you start or assign it, open Accommodations. Which setting would help one of your learners: read aloud, reduce answer choices, or extra time?

03
Wayground~15 min

Presentation (+2 question types)

You'll make: one of your own decks as an interactive lesson, with at least two different question types between the slides.

  1. In Wayground, click Create and choose Presentation.
  2. Upload one of your own decks. No deck to hand? Use the Digital Footprint sample.
  3. Let the AI suggest questions between your slides, then check and edit each one.
  4. Add at least two different question types of your own. Use the demo lesson menu for ideas: a poll to open, a categorise or hotspot in the middle, a draw exit ticket.
  5. Preview it as a learner, then Start now to run it live or Assign it.

Work in Google Slides? The Wayground for Google Slides add-on brings questions into Slides itself.

Inclusion challenge

Choose one question type that doesn't rely on reading, like hotspot or draw. Which learner gets a way in that a written answer wouldn't give them?

Wayground~15 min

Video

You'll make: a short video from your subject with questions that pop up while learners watch.

Sample video: search for one from your own subject, or use this one: youtube.com/watch?v=jflyh6XroA0

  1. Search YouTube for a short video from your own subject that you'd use with a group: a demonstration, a real-world application, a safety clip. No time to search? Use the sample video instead.
  2. In Wayground, click Create and choose Video, then paste the link.
  3. Trim it to the part you need.
  4. Add 2 or 3 questions at the key moments. Mix in one open-ended question that asks learners to reflect.
  5. Preview it, then assign it to a class.

Just want a quiz about the video? Use Create → Assessment → Generate with AI → YouTube and paste the link.

Inclusion challenge

Learners can pause and rewatch at their own pace. Who in your group would benefit most: ESOL learners, someone who missed the practical, or a learner with slower processing?

05
Wayground~15 min

Passage

You'll make: a reading activity, such as a case study, a health and safety notice or a job description, with questions next to the text.

  1. In Wayground, click Create and choose Passage.
  2. Paste in a short text from your subject, or ask the AI to write one on your topic at the right reading level.
  3. Add questions that check understanding of the text. Let the AI suggest some, then edit.
  4. Preview it as a learner and listen to it with read aloud.
  5. Assign it to a class.

Inclusion challenge

Passages are where reading support shines: read aloud, dyslexia font, translate, bilingual dictionary and reading mode. Which one would one of your learners switch on?

Wayground~10 min

Flashcards

You'll make: a set of key terms for a unit, such as tool names, trade vocabulary or command words, that learners can revise in their own time.

  1. In Wayground, click Create and choose Flashcards.
  2. Add 8 to 10 terms with short definitions, or let the AI generate a set from your topic and edit it.
  3. Add an image to the terms that are easier to recognise than describe.
  4. Assign the set for self-study before or after the lesson.

Inclusion challenge

Pre-teach the vocabulary before the lesson. Which ESOL or EAL learner would arrive more confident if they'd met the key words first, with translation on?

Stretch

Use the same terms to build a match question in a quiz (tile 02).

Stretch & challenge

Finished early, or carrying on after the session? Try one of these.

Set an accommodation

Assign your activity and open Accommodations. Try read aloud or reduce answer choices on a test account, not a real learner.

Bring your class across

In Wayground, go to Students → Classes and import from Google Classroom. Then you just assign, with no codes to share.

Install the Chrome extension

Build questions from any web page, article or Google Doc. Get the Wayground AI extension.

Make differentiated versions

Create an easier and a harder version of the same activity. Watch how.

Build an AI rubric

Let Wayground mark open answers against criteria you set. Watch how.

No devices? No problem

Open any activity, click Download as worksheet, then photograph completed sheets in the Wayground Teacher app to mark them.
