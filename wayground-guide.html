Wayground quick guide · about 10 minutes

The whole Wayground loop

Everything you need to run your first activity with a group. Wayground is the platform formerly known as Quizizz, and every lecturer has full Premium access through the college.

The short version

  1. Log in at wayground.com with your college email.
  2. Import your classes from Google Classroom.
  3. Search the Library for your topic and save something.
  4. Assign it to a class.
  5. Check the report afterwards.

That's the whole loop. Everything below is detail you can pick up as you go.

1. Log in for the first time

  1. Go to wayground.com and click Log in.
  2. Sign in with your college email address.
  3. Used Quizizz before on this email? Use your existing password. Your account has already been upgraded to Premium and nothing has been lost.
  4. Never used it? Click Sign up, choose Teacher, and use your college email.

Check you're on Premium: if you can see the full Create menu and the Library in the left-hand menu, you're on the college plan. No invitation email? That's normal: existing accounts are upgraded quietly.

2. Find something ready-made

The fastest way to start is not to create anything at all.

  1. Click Library in the left-hand menu.
  2. Search for your topic, for example "health and safety at work" or "fractions".
  3. Click a result to preview it.
  4. Click Save to add it to your own library, or Start to use it straight away.

You can edit anything you save: change questions, delete the ones you don't want, add your own.

3. Create your own: five ways

Pick whichever suits what you already have. Always check what the AI produces before you use it.

A

From a topic

  1. Create → Assessment
  2. Enter a topic, or paste in text.
  3. Choose the level, number of questions and output language.
  4. Click Create resource, review, then Publish.

Full instructions

B

From a worksheet or past paper

  1. Create → Assessment → Generate with AI → Document
  2. Upload a PDF, Word file, PowerPoint or a photo of a worksheet.
  3. Review and click Publish.

Full instructions

C

From a website or YouTube video

  1. Create → Assessment → Generate with AI
  2. Choose Website or YouTube and paste the link.
  3. Review and click Publish.

Full instructions

D

The Chrome extension: quickest of all

  1. Install the extension and pin it (puzzle-piece icon → pin).
  2. On any web page, article, video or Google Doc, click it.
  3. Choose subject, level, number and types of question, then Create questions.
E

From scratch

  1. Create → Assessment → Create from scratch
  2. Choose a question type, type it, mark the correct answers.
  3. Click + Add question for the next, then Publish.

After two questions, Wayground suggests similar ones you can add with one click.

4. Set up your classes once

This saves the most time. Once learners are in a class, you never share a code again: you assign, and the work appears in their accounts.

Import from Google Classroom (recommended)

  1. Click Students in the left-hand menu, then the Classes tab.
  2. Choose to import from Google Classroom.
  3. Sign in with your college Google account when asked.
  4. Select your classes. Learners come in automatically.

Or create a class by hand

  1. Students → Classes → Create class, and name it, for example "L2 Construction".
  2. Click Add student and share the join link or class code once.
  3. Learners go to wayground.com/join/class. Codes last up to 14 days, so make a new one for late joiners.

5. Give the activity to your learners

Live, in the lesson

  1. Open the activity and click Start now.
  2. Choose the session mode and settings, then Start.
  3. Learners join at wayground.com/join with the code on screen, or by scanning the QR code.

Independently, in their own time

  1. Open the activity and click Assign.
  2. Select your class and set a deadline.
  3. Choose attempts, timers and so on, then Assign.

Full instructions

6. See how your learners did

  1. Click Sessions in the left-hand menu.
  2. Open the activity you ran.

You'll see each learner's score, accuracy, completion, time per question, and a question-by-question breakdown of who got what wrong. Reports break results down by question and topic, so you can spot a misconception across the group and tackle it next session rather than at assessment.

Evidencing inclusive practice? Reports show what support was provided and how each learner performed, which helps for learners with an EHCP or additional learning support.

Full instructions

7. Support learners who need it

Give the whole group the same activity while adjusting the experience for individual learners: read aloud, a dyslexia-friendly font, translation, extra time, fewer answer choices and focus mode. Set them per learner, once, and they apply every time.

Look for Accommodations on the settings page when you start or assign an activity. See the full list.

Check the college's data guidance before entering real learner SEND details.

8. If learners don't have devices

  1. Open any activity and click Download as worksheet. Choose graded or non-graded.
  2. Print it, and learners answer by hand.
  3. Photograph the completed sheets with the Wayground Teacher app.
  4. Wayground marks them and the results appear in your dashboard.

This works for multiple choice, fill in the blank and open-ended questions. For longer answers, upload a rubric so it knows how to mark them.
